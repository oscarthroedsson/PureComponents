Text inputs support various HTML5 input types: text, email, password, tel, and number.

Text inputs support three sizes: small (sm), medium (md), and large (lg).

Text inputs support different border radius styles: sharp, smooth, and rounded.

Error states require aria-invalid="true" and aria-describedby pointing to the error message.

Disabled inputs use the native disabled attribute.

All text inputs must have proper labels and ARIA attributes for screen reader support.
