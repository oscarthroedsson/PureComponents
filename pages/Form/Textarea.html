Textareas support three sizes: small (sm), medium (md), and large (lg).

Textareas support different resize behaviors: vertical (default), horizontal, both, and none.

Textareas can display character count using aria-describedby and aria-live="polite".

Error states require aria-invalid="true" and aria-describedby pointing to the error message.

Textareas can be disabled using the native disabled attribute.

Textareas must have proper labels and ARIA attributes for screen reader support.
