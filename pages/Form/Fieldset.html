No border. A fieldset is a semantic frame — the legend names the group and is read before every control inside it.

panel draws a whole surface — fill, border, radius and padding — not a hairline. A bare border would sit flush with the rest of the form while the fields inside were pushed in by the padding, and the group would look narrower for no visible reason.

<fieldset disabled> disables the whole subtree for real — the controls match :disabled, leave the tab order and are left out of the submitted data. This file writes nothing for it. It is the reason the component is a native fieldset and not a div with role="group".

The size goes on the group and every field in it follows.

A fieldset has min-width: min-content in the UA sheet and refuses to shrink below its widest child. min-inline-size: 0 in the base is what keeps this one inside its 200px grid column instead of bursting out of it.
