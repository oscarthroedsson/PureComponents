.item-row lays media out beside the text. The media keeps its box however long the text runs, and the text shrinks instead of pushing the row wider than the list.

.item-title is the strong line, .item-text the secondary one. The rank, the cover and the play button are the page's, not the component's.

Same component, different contents. The avatars are the page's own and read --list-media-size, so they follow the list's size class.

The class goes on the <ul> or <ol>. Every <li> becomes a row on its own — padding, and a hairline between it and the next. The last row has none, so the list ends on content.

list-items-none removes padding from the direct <li> elements when another PureUI layout class owns their placement. The list keeps its semantics and optional rules.

list-top, list-center (default) and list-bottom decide how the media lines up against text that runs to more than one line.

data-interactive gives a direct item a flat hover surface. Its one .list-action extends across the row without adding Card elevation; secondary controls remain reachable. list-ghost removes the permanent rules so the interaction surfaces carry the separation.

list-sm, list-md (default) and list-lg move item padding, type size and media size together — the avatars follow because they read --list-media-size.
