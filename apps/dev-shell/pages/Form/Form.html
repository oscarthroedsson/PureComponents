Nothing is styled on the form itself. It spaces its children and sets the scale for everything under it. This is the whole library with no tuning at all.

.pu-form.lg sets the scale once. The fieldset inside it says sm and wins for its own subtree; the last field says sm and wins over both. Nearest declaration wins, at any depth — no descendant selector anywhere in the family.

A group retunes its subtree; a leaf changes only itself. Put lg on the field and the label grows with the control. Put it on the input and only the input grows — which is the difference between the two, made visible.

Same mechanism, same rules — one variable, set once on the form.

Every variable in the channel can be set on the form and reaches every control below it. No control is named, and no selector is written.
