The host is the target by default. Hold the button with a pointer or keyboard to drive --motion-progress from 0 to 1.

Hover or focus each button. Slide names describe the direction travelled into the resting position.

The trigger stays on the parent while every direct child reads the inherited progress. Tab into the group to test focus-within.

overlay keeps the label after the collection still. reflow grows the collection’s real gap and moves the label, without reserving that space before hover.

pressed reads the semantic aria-pressed state. Click to toggle it; motion does not invent a parallel active state.

Timing and distance are variables, so a consumer can retune an existing effect without adding a selector.
